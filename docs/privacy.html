Privacy statement

gsic, last updated August 2026

gsic does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. The application has no network functionality of any kind.

What the application does with your files

Images you choose to open are read from your device. The compressed results are written back to your device, either beside the original or to a folder you pick. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and no copy is retained outside the location you selected.

What the application does not do

There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising, no account system, and no automatic update check. The application makes no outbound connections, so there is nothing for it to send.

Third party components

gsic is built with open source libraries for windowing, the user interface, image reading, and compression. None of them are configured to contact any server, and no third party receives any information about you.

Contact

Questions about this statement can be raised on the project's issue tracker.
